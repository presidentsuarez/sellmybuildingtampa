Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Who we are

Suarez Global Ventures LLC is a direct buyer of buildings in Tampa Bay. This policy covers sellmybuildingtampa.com. "We" and "us" mean Suarez Global Ventures LLC.

What we collect

When you fill out our form, we collect:

  • Your name, phone number and email
  • The property address and property type
  • Your timeline, if you share it
  • The consent text you saw, the time you submitted, and the page you were on
  • Your browser type and a one-way scrambled (hashed) form of your IP address, used to stop spam and abuse. We do not store your raw IP address.
  • Marketing tags in the page link, if present: utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, utm_term and utm_content

Our form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to check that you are a person and not a bot. Cloudflare may process information from your browser and device to do this, under Cloudflare's Turnstile privacy terms.

How we use it

We use your info to contact you about your property by call, text or email, and to review your property and make you an offer. Calls and texts may use automated means. Consent is not a condition of any sale.

We don't sell your info

We do not sell your personal info. We do not share it with other buyers or marketers.

Who we share it with

We share your info only with service providers who help us run our business, such as our CRM and database host, our email provider, and Cloudflare for spam protection. They may use it only to provide those services to us.

Text messages

Msg and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. Reply STOP to any text to opt out. Reply HELP for help, or call (813) 544-5781.

How long we keep it

We keep the info you send until you ask us to delete it. Call or email us anytime to have it deleted: (813) 544-5781. The hashed IP address and email we use for spam checks are deleted once they are more than 2 days old. This cleanup runs the next time someone submits the form.

Deleting your info

To ask us to delete your info, call (813) 544-5781. We will confirm when it is done, unless the law requires us to keep it.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
